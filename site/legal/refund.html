Refund Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Jackdaw Mail Pro payments are processed by Paddle as the merchant of record. You can ask for a refund within 14 days of any payment, for any reason.

1. 14-day refund

If you are not satisfied with Jackdaw Mail Pro, you can request a full refund of a subscription payment within 14 days of the date of that payment. This applies to the first payment and to each renewal, for both the monthly ($9.99) and the yearly ($79.99) plan. Taxes charged on the payment are refunded together with it.

2. How to request a refund

  • Use the link in the Paddle receipt e-mail, or open paddle.net, enter the e-mail address used at checkout and choose the transaction.
  • Or write through the Jackdaw Mail issue tracker. Do not post license keys, passwords or mail contents in a public issue; state only the date of the payment and ask for contact details to be exchanged privately.

Refunds are issued by Paddle to the original payment method. The time it takes to appear on a statement depends on the bank or payment provider.

3. What happens to access

A full refund ends Pro access for the refunded subscription: the license key is revoked and the Pro features switch off. The free features, connected accounts and local data are not affected.

4. Cancellation without a refund

You can cancel a subscription at any time through the subscription controls in the Paddle receipt. Cancellation stops future renewals; Pro access remains available until the end of the period already paid. Payments older than 14 days are not refunded unless required by the law that applies to you.

5. Your statutory rights

This policy does not limit rights that cannot be waived under the consumer law applicable to you. See also the Paddle Buyer Terms and the Terms of Use.
