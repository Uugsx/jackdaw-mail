## 3. Products sold on Lemon Squeezy

Jackdaw Mail is a desktop mail, calendar and contacts client for Exchange/OWA, EWS, ActiveSync, IMAP/JMAP and CardDAV/CalDAV accounts. It is intended for individual professionals, support teams and organizations that want a local mail workflow with clear response-time tracking.

The paid product is **Jackdaw Mail Pro**, a digital software entitlement with two recurring subscription variants:

- **Monthly:** $9.99 per month;
- **Yearly:** $79.99 per year.

Pro unlocks interactive response-time reports, a live unanswered-mail/SLA queue, weekday working hours, working-minute reminders, HTML report export, formatted HTML signatures, multiple accounts and supported shared mailboxes. The first mailbox remains available in the free version. A seven-day Pro trial is also available without a payment card and requires online validation.

Jackdaw Mail is developed and maintained by Uugsx. The public repository contains open-source code under EUPL-1.2 together with proprietary modules and third-party dependencies governed by their respective licenses. Pro is a subscription entitlement to use the paid features; it is not a custom-development or consulting service.

Fulfilment is fully automatic. After checkout, Lemon Squeezy processes the payment and sends the license key and receipt to the purchase email. The customer downloads the installer from GitHub Releases, enters the key in `Settings → Jackdaw Pro`, and the license service automatically verifies the key and returns a signed entitlement. No manual customization or manual provisioning is required.

The app connects directly to the customer’s selected mail provider and stores mail data locally. The license service does not receive mail bodies, attachments, passwords, calendars, contacts or address books.
